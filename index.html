How invites work

Each store handles testing its own way. Here is exactly what happens on your phone.

Android

  1. Sign up below with the email address on your Google account.
  2. We add you to the Play Store test and email your private invite link.
  3. Install from the Play Store, then keep and use the app through the 14-day test window. Google only counts testers who install from the Store and stay in.

Android invites open as soon as Google finishes reviewing the developer account. Sign up now to hold your spot.

iPhone

  1. Sign up below with your Apple ID email address.
  2. Your invite arrives through TestFlight, Apple's free testing app.
  3. Tap the invite, install, and you are in. Updates arrive in TestFlight as new test builds land.

iPhone invites open once the Apple Developer enrollment is complete. Sign up now and you are first in line.

Before you share your email

Your email address is used for one thing only: sending your testing invite. It is never added to a marketing list, never shared, never sold. Want out? Uninstall the app, reply to any invite email, and we remove you from the tester list. That is the whole policy.
